I am a researcher at the Computational Neurobiology Laboratory (CNL) at the Salk Institute, led by Prof. Terry Sejnowski. I obtained my undergraduate degree in Computer Science from University of Düsseldorf in 2022 and an M.S. in Computer Science from UC Davis in 2025.

My research interests include machine learning, deep learning, and computational neuroscience.
