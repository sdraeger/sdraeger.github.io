# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, prospective collaborators, students, and technical hiring committees who need a quick, credible view of Simon Dräger's research interests and selected projects. Most visitors arrive from a paper, GitHub profile, application, or direct introduction and want useful evidence without navigating a large academic site.

## Product Purpose

Present Simon's research profile and selected work in a compact personal website. Success means the homepage communicates his areas of work immediately, research projects can be added as independent Markdown files, and every page remains legible and credible on desktop and mobile.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exact, and curious. The site should feel like a carefully maintained technical notebook: restrained enough for academic work, but more deliberate and personal than a generic academic theme.

## Anti-references

Avoid the stock al-folio appearance, oversized centered hero text, decorative dashboards, dense navigation, generic card grids, copied Svelte Agentation branding, fabricated project claims, and unnecessary frontend dependencies.

## Design Principles

1. Let research content establish credibility.
2. Keep the reading column compact and calm.
3. Make personal copy and projects easy to update independently.
4. Use interaction only to support reading, copying, and navigation.
5. Preserve evidence and attribution without visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, touch-friendly controls, and useful layouts from narrow mobile screens through large desktops.
